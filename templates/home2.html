About the Project

Our project is designed to predict crop prices accurately by leveraging dynamic dropdowns and machine learning. This platform enables farmers and traders to make informed decisions by providing district-specific market insights, crop variety options, and predictive pricing data..

Our Features

1. Crop Price Prediction: Utilize advanced machine learning algorithms for accurate price forecasting.

2. Dynamic Dropdowns: Seamless navigation through interconnected district, market, commodity, and variety options.

3. Real-Time Insights: Immediate results based on user inputs and up-to-date market trends.
